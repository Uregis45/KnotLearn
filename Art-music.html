Art & Music Basics

🎨

What is Art?

Art is a way of expressing ideas and feelings. People use art to show creativity and culture.

🖌️ Types of Art:

  • Drawing
  • Painting
  • Sculpture
  • Crafts
📌 Example:

Painting a landscape or drawing a portrait.

🎵

What is Music?

Music is the art of sound. It uses rhythm, melody, and instruments to create emotion.

🎸 Types of Music:

  • Traditional music
  • Classical music
  • Pop music
  • Gospel music
📌 Example:

Singing, playing piano, guitar, or drums.
