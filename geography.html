What is Geography?

Geography is the study of the Earth.
It helps us understand places, people, climate, and environment.

📌 Did you know?

The word "geography" comes from Greek words meaning "to write about the Earth."

🏔️

Physical Geography

Physical geography studies natural features of the Earth.

🌋 Natural Features:

  • Mountains
  • Rivers
  • Oceans
  • Deserts
📌 Did you know?

Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth.

👥

Human Geography

Human geography studies people and their activities.

🏘️ Human Features:

  • Population
  • Cities
  • Culture
  • Transport
📌 Did you know?

Cities grow because of jobs and trade.
