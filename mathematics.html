Addition

Addition means putting numbers together to find the total.

📌 Example:

2 + 3 = 5
➖

Subtraction

Subtraction means taking one number away from another.

📌 Example:

7 − 4 = 3
✖️

Multiplication

Multiplication means repeated addition — a faster way to add groups.

📌 Example:

3 × 4 = 12
➗

Division

Division means sharing equally into groups.

📌 Example:

12 ÷ 3 = 4
