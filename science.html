📚 Science Topics

Learn step by step with simple explanations

🌱

Living Things

Living things can grow, move, breathe, and reproduce.

📌 Examples:

  • Humans
  • Animals
  • Plants
🪨

Non-Living Things

Non-living things do not grow or breathe.

📌 Examples:

  • Stone
  • Water
  • Chair
💧

States of Matter

Matter exists in three main states.

📌 Examples:

  • Solid: Ice
  • Liquid: Water
  • Gas: Air
⚡

Energy

Energy helps us do work and move.

📌 Examples:

  • Light energy
  • Heat energy
  • Sound energy
