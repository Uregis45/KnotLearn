What is History?

History is the study of past events.
It helps us understand how people lived, worked, and changed the world.

📌 Did you know?

History helps us learn from the past to build a better future.

Ancient Civilizations

Ancient civilizations were the first advanced societies.
Examples include Egypt, Mesopotamia, and Indus Valley.

📌 Did you know?

Ancient Egyptians built the pyramids.

Middle Ages

The Middle Ages came after ancient times.
Castles, kings, and knights were common in this period.

📌 Did you know?

Knights wore armor for protection.
